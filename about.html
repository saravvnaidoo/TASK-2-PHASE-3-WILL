THE BEGINNING

23THE YEAR WE OPENED

Good gear should be within reach.

Jason Naidoo started Next Level Gaming & E-Sports Arena in 2023 with a straightforward idea: local players shouldn’t need to buy an expensive PC or console just to enjoy a great gaming session.

He built a welcoming space where friends could meet, teams could practise and anyone could sit down at high-spec PCs, current-gen consoles and quality peripherals. Come for one match or make a day of it. The setup is ready either way.
